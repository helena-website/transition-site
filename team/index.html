Helena Foulkes is a Rhode Islander and a business leader who spent her career running large organizations, including as president of CVS Pharmacy and later as chief executive of Hudson's Bay Company.

She ran for governor to make state government work better for the people who rely on it: families looking for housing they can afford, students and workers looking for opportunity, and businesses looking for a state that says yes.

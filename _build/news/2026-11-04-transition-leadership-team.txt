PROVIDENCE, RI: Governor-elect Helena Foulkes today announced the leadership team that will guide her transition through Inauguration Day.

The team includes Caitlin Molina, Transition Executive Director; RJ Kermes, Transition Deputy Director, Operations; Jon Romano, Senior Advisor; and Jon Carvalho, Communications Lead.

Rhode Islanders can share ideas or express interest in serving at www.helenatransition.com.

Media contact: [press@helenatransition.com](mailto:press@helenatransition.com)

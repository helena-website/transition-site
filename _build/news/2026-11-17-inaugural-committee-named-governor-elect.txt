title: Inaugural Committee Named for Governor-elect
date: 2026-11-17

summary: Members from across Rhode Island will plan and host the gubernatorial inauguration and related events in January 2027, when Governor-elect Foulkes is officially sworn in as the 77th Governor of the State of Rhode Island
---
PROVIDENCE – The Inaugural Committee and leadership team for the inauguration of Governor-elect Helena Buonanno Foulkes was announced today by the Foulkes Transition Team.

Eight co-chairs will lead the Committee: Lisa Andoscia, Donna Paolino Coia, Frank DiGiammarino, Dr. Saira Hussein, Julie Owens, Senator Ana Quezada, Navyn Salem, and Michael Stephens.

Nora Barre will serve as Executive Director of the Inaugural Committee, and Kate Ramstad will be the Committee’s Finance Director.

The Inaugural Committee will plan and host the gubernatorial inauguration and related events in January 2027, when Governor-elect Foulkes is officially sworn in as the 77th Governor of the State of Rhode Island.

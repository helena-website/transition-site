title: Meet the Governor-elect: Foulkes to Hold Listening Sessions Across the State
date: 2026-11-20
type: Press release
summary: Governor-elect Helena Buonanno Foulkes will hold a series of listening sessions across the state to hear directly from Rhode Islanders.
---
PROVIDENCE – Governor-elect Helena Buonanno Foulkes will hold a series of listening sessions across the state to hear directly from Rhode Islanders about their ideas for how state government can work better, hopes for the future administration, and suggestions about how to fix what isn’t working well now.

The Governor-elect has received more than 1,200 ideas from residents through her transition team’s website, [helenatransition.com](http://helenatransition.com/), where Rhode Islanders are encouraged to share their recommendations for the incoming administration.

[Four listening sessions](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/R3VT5sUHBx) have been announced for the coming weeks:

- Providence - Tuesday, November 24, 2026: 5:00-7:00 p.m. at the Elmwood Community Center, 75 Atlantic Avenue, Providence
- Aquidneck Island - Monday, November 30, 2026: 5:00-7:00 p.m. at the Newport County Campus of CCRI, 1 John H Chafee Boulevard, Newport
- Warwick - Tuesday, December 1, 2026: 5:00-7:00 p.m. at the Central Library of the Warwick Public Library, 600 Sandy Lane, Warwick
- Woonsocket - Wednesday, December 2, 2026: 5:00-7:00 p.m. at the Woonsocket Education Center, 115 Main Street, Woonsocket

Rhode Islanders are invited to attend to share their ideas with the Governor-elect and her team. Anyone interested in attending can [RSVP here](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/R3VT5sUHBx).

## Governor-elect Helena Buonanno Foulkes Transition

Governor-elect Foulkes has launched a website for Rhode Islanders to share ideas, express interest in public service, and to serve as a resource for news and plans for the Governor-elect’s incoming administration, at [helenatransition.com](http://helenatransition.com/).
